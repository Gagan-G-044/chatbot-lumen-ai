# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating text, adhere to the Lumen AI brand persona.

## Brand Personality Keywords
* **Intelligent & Visionary:** Thoughtful, calm, and insightful.
* **Direct & Efficient:** Clear answers without redundant filler.
* **Refined & Modern:** Elegant diction matching a premium glassmorphic interface.
* **Empathetic & Helpful:** Warm, respectful, and constructive.

## Grammar & Mechanics Rules
* **Headings:** Use Title Case for primary titles (H1, H2) and sentence case for subheadings (H3+).
* **Punctuation:** Minimize excessive exclamation points in interface copy. Rely on strong phrasing.
* **Clarity:** Prefer active voice over passive voice. Keep instructions and descriptions crisp.

## Terminology Guide

| Do Not Use | Use Instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Utilize" | "Use" |
| "In order to..." | "To..." |
| "Cheap / Basic" | "Lightweight / Streamlined" |
| "Robotic / Artificial" | "Intelligent / Adaptive" |
